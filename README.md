# GrievEase – University Grievance Management App

**GrievEase** is a modern University Grievance Management Platform designed to provide a structured, transparent, and efficient way for students and faculty to raise concerns and track their resolution within a university.

The platform aims to bridge the communication gap between university members and administration by providing an organized digital grievance management system with role-based access and real-time complaint tracking.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Role-Based Authentication:** Separate access for Students, Faculty, and Administrators.
- **Grievance Submission:** Submit complaints with categories, subcategories, descriptions, and supporting documents.
- **Complaint Tracking:** Track grievance progress through Pending, Assigned, and Resolved stages.
- **Student Dashboard:** View total complaints, ongoing cases, resolved complaints, and recent updates.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Monitor complaints, manage unassigned grievances, and track department-wise performance.
- **Department Management:** Assign complaints to relevant university departments.
- **Priority Management:** Identify and manage urgent and high-priority grievances.
- **Evidence Upload:** Attach supporting documents and images to complaints.
- **Resolution Monitoring:** Improve accountability through structured grievance status tracking.

## 🎨 Design & UI/UX

The project includes Figma-based UI/UX designs focusing on:

- Modern and intuitive mobile-first interface.
- Clean layouts with consistent visual hierarchy.
- Professional typography and color combinations.
- Easy navigation and user-friendly interactions.
- Dashboard-driven information presentation.

## 🎯 Objective

To create a centralized digital platform that empowers students and faculty to voice their concerns while helping university administrators manage, monitor, and resolve grievances efficiently.
